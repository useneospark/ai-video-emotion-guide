## What is Emotion Direction?

**Emotion direction** is a prompting style that describes only *visible acting behavior*: eyes, brows, mouth, breath, shoulders, in temporal order.

The principle: **keep performance separate from production**. Room, camera, wardrobe, story, audio and duration live in your own scene prompt — the direction covers only the emotion, and appends after any scene description.

Every direction ships with a reference performance generated by Seedance 2.5.

> Want to generate emotional AI video performances directly? **[NeoSpark](https://useneospark.com/?ref=ai-video-emotion-guide)** supports Seedance-class video models out of the box.

---

## How to Use

1. Pick an emotion by family and intensity.
2. Copy its performance prompt.
3. Paste it after your own scene description and trigger, then generate on NeoSpark.

### Combination Example

Your scene description + emotion direction:

```
A young woman in a rainy neon-lit alley, medium close-up.
The eyebrows stay knitted, the eyes flick rapidly from side to side, the lower lip pulls between the teeth, and breathing remains shallow and quick. The hands grip together as the weight keeps shifting.
```
